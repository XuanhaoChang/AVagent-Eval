02 / THE PROBLEM

One video.
Many ways to
fall short.

A generated clip is more than a sequence of frames. It has a request, visible subjects, spoken words, on-screen text, and timing.

DefectLens is a tool-augmented agent that retrieves atomic defect events and localizes them for inspection. It combines broad visual proposals with speech, text, speaker, metadata, and synchronization observations. Modality-specific verification grounds candidate claims, while consolidation preserves independent findings and their locations.

Shared observations can support multiple checking dimensions, directing additional inspection toward specific claims.

03 / APPROACH

From request to report.

Follow the evidence
through four steps.

01

Propose

Inspect the request, references, and timestamped frames for candidate discrepancies.

02

Gather

Build reusable observations from frames, crops, speech, text, motion, and media properties.

03

Verify

Check each claim against the observations relevant to its modality and requirement.

04

Localize

Preserve independent findings with their available intervals, keyframes, and bounding boxes.

✳

Evidence travels with the finding. A visual issue may include a keyframe and box; an audio issue may be localized to a time interval.
